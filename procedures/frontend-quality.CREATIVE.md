# Creative direction workflow

Load this reference for `explore` and for consequential expressive `evolve` work. Its purpose is directed distinctiveness: widening the search enough to find a memorable direction, selecting it against the user task and identity brief, and only then applying the shared restraint and delivery rules. It does not make novelty, media, or a Critic mandatory for routine interface work.

## Discover: form a taste-bearing brief

Extend the frontend hypothesis with:

- the intended feeling or response and the product quality it should reinforce;
- identity constraints that must remain recognizable, including the active interface family for `evolve`;
- a candidate signature idea expressed as a compositional, material, typographic, imagery, or interaction principle;
- user- or project-supplied references when available, treated as a quality bar or moodboard rather than a copying target; and
- anti-goals: impressions, conventions, or visual behaviors that would make this product feel wrong.

When important taste or interaction choices remain open, create a small number of full-surface directions that differ in composition, typography, visual material, imagery, information reveal, or interaction character. Choose enough contrast to resolve the uncertainty; palette swaps alone rarely help. A clear supplied direction or already-resolved choice may proceed directly to one prototype. Keep exploratory code outside production imports and preserve the task, truthful content, required states, and privacy boundary across comparisons.

Use the brief, domain structure, observations, or references to widen the search. Inspiration is not evidence or authority; discard ideas that conflict with task success, accessibility, feasibility, or protected product semantics.

## Define: render, compare, and select

Render the selected prototype or each compared direction as a complete surface at the primary viewport with realistic, privacy-safe content. Compare task clarity, brief fit, identity coherence, continuity, execution craft, responsive feasibility, and obvious template defaults. A direction does not win merely because it is unusual.

For `explore`, and for consequential expressive `evolve` work when an independent reviewer is available and proportional, use a visual Critic in a fresh context. Give it the brief, active continuity constraints, rendered screenshots, and optional references. Withhold source code, implementation effort, earlier critiques, the implementer's preferred verdict, and any hidden answer. Ask for a ranking, the strongest and weakest reasons for each direction, and a short list of material gaps. A Critic is advisory; the root agent retains synthesis and the user or named project authority retains consequential taste decisions.

Run one initial critique and at most one re-critique after addressing the highest-value gaps. Do not loop toward an arbitrary numeric score. Stop when the bounded pass is complete, improvement no longer converges, or the remaining choice is an owner preference; surface the unresolved choice instead of spending indefinitely.

Choose one direction before production implementation. Record why it best balances the intended response, primary task, continuity, and feasibility, plus what was deliberately rejected. When direction approval is required, it applies only to the rendered revision shown; do not request separate implementation approval when the task already authorizes it.

## Media checkpoint

Ask whether purpose-built imagery, texture, illustration, 3D, or motion would materially carry the selected identity better than code-only decoration. When it would and an appropriate runtime capability is available, use the relevant media-generation or asset workflow deliberately rather than defaulting to gradients, generic shapes, or placeholder icons.

Generated or sourced media needs a named role, repository-safe provenance, responsive sizing or cropping, loading and performance treatment, accessible alternative text or decorative hiding, and a non-media fallback where the task depends on it. Video or complex motion is exceptional: require a user-serving purpose, a static and reduced-motion fallback, a performance budget, and rendered interaction evidence. Never request credentials in chat or place provider secrets or secret locations in durable design instructions.

## Deliver: converge without erasing the identity

Implement the selected direction through the active project design system and the `frontend-quality` observe–reason–change–reobserve loop. Preserve task success, content truth, keyboard operation, accessibility, responsive behavior, supported states, and registered-family continuity according to the selected posture.

Then perform the reduction pass. Remove effects, containers, labels, icons, media, or motion that do not carry the task or chosen identity. Retain one or two recognizable signature ideas and execute them consistently instead of accumulating unrelated flourishes.

Finish with an AI-pattern check: could a major visual decision be replaced by a common template default without changing the product's meaning or intended response? If so, the identity is still underdefined. Treat familiar arrangements, gradients, glass effects, cards, pills, oversized type, or ornamental graphics as diagnostic prompts rather than universal bans; each can be valid when the brief and product give it a specific job.

Record the posture, brief, direction axes, rendered comparison, selection rationale, Critic result when used, media decision, reduction, deterministic proof, and remaining verification gaps. Do not claim improved design capability from prose compliance or one attractive screenshot; representative rendered comparisons belong to the frontend-quality evaluation suite.
