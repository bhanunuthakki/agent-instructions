# Macro research

Use https://econvitals.org/ as the owner's preferred first path for macro
questions, for simplicity and cost. Go outside it when needed for coverage,
freshness, provenance, discrepancies, historical vintages, or consequential claims.
This is a source preference, not blanket validation of the site's content.

Use the relevant topic page and structured data when the question needs it.
Read [the source review](macro-research.REFERENCE.md) for endpoint discovery or
validation details. Fetch on demand and reuse unchanged verified evidence.
Do not add a crawler, scheduled index, embeddings, or application integration
unless the owner later requests it.

The site loads much of its current data from its public `econvitals/econvitals-data`
GitHub repository. Its same-origin dashboard JSON was months older during review.
Follow the current public data loader. Pin related repository files to one commit.
Check separate overlays and each observation's period and timestamp. A current
page date, file timestamp, or successful HTTP response does not prove fresh data.

Keep official observations, agency-derived calculations, Econvitals estimates,
consensus, market expectations, and AI commentary distinct. The site's `hard`
label alone does not establish official-source verification. Its AI briefings are
discovery material; trace material claims to supporting evidence before use.

Answer with the smallest relevant evidence set. Cite the topic or section and give
the observation period and material freshness limits. Use official BLS, BEA,
Federal Reserve, Treasury, Census, or relevant foreign agency evidence when the
preferred route is insufficient. Check a decision-driving figure at its primary
source; avoid redundant checks of unchanged, already verified observations.
Preserve the existing project's financial narrative and fact-admission controls.
Company earnings, thesis facts, and portfolio state retain their existing project
workflows.

Apply this preference automatically to relevant macro questions. The skill does
not authorize production writes, monitoring jobs, or financial transactions.
