# Writing and Delivery

Use this reference only when the user asks for the submitted memo or final communication. Start from the analyses and point of view retained during pruning. Confirm material constraints such as audience, length, format, and required questions. Do not reopen broad exploration merely to make the memo look complete.

## The memo's job

The memo is not a narration of the workbook. It gives an experienced operator the conclusion, evidence that matters, and actions to take. A short memo may include a headline, a few findings, the central implication required by the brief, specific actions, concise limitations, and an honest AI methodology when requested. Combine or remove repeated sections.

## Plain-language standard

Write directly and use first person for judgment or process. Avoid consultant slogans, inflated descriptions of calculations, unsupported certainty, symmetrical prose when the evidence points one way, and parades of numbers with no decision attached. Do not use em dashes. Sound like a finance-fluent peer.

## Quantitative discipline

Use numbers to establish magnitude, mix, trend, concentration, or economics. Limit the memo to figures the reader should remember. For every material claim, confirm its period and definition, reproduce it independently when practical, distinguish levels from changes and gross from net, and state the eligible population when sample size matters.

Link each material claim to the exact supporting sheet and range. Recheck deep links after workbook changes.

## Implications and action

Explicitly answer the decision, recommendation, or assessment requested by the brief. Name the relevant driver or tradeoff, why it matters economically, what is observed versus inferred, and what data are missing.

Actions should be operational. Name the cadence, owner, population, decision, and horizon when relevant. Monitoring is not a complete action unless the response to the signal is clear. Recommend a test or next analysis when evidence does not support a broad policy change.

## Final checks

Keep the requested page limit, preserve useful owner wording, cut duplicate analysis before shrinking layout, remove placeholders and stale figures, verify hyperlinks and sharing, and export once to check pagination. Describe AI use specifically and truthfully without implying it replaced judgment.
