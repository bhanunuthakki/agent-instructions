# Review and Fluency

Use this reference for final audits, submission decisions, and interview preparation.

## Model audit

Trace important outputs from memo to output tab, canonical build, assumptions, and raw source. Review formulas across copied ranges. Check timing boundaries, lifecycle definitions, population consistency, direct links versus duplicated calculations, active assumptions, attribution and contractual bases, references after structural changes, named ranges, charts, hyperlinks, masked missing data, and formatting consistency.

Reconcile major totals independently from source tabs. A check comparing two downstream formulas is not independent evidence.

## Narrative audit

Read the memo without the workbook. Confirm the main conclusion is obvious, the brief's central question is answered, actions address the conclusion, facts and interpretations are separated, caveats appear once, secondary metrics are limited, every linked range supports its claim, and the AI methodology reflects the real process. Cut accurate paragraphs that do not help the conclusion or action.

## Submission decision

Recommend submission when required deliverables are present, the central answer is explicit, material claims reconcile, checks pass, limitations are labeled, outputs are readable, the memo fits the requested format, and links work for a non-owner. Do not recommend more analysis merely because it is possible. After the files are frozen, make only corrections or changes that materially improve the answer.

## Fluency handoff

Prepare a concise defense around a 60-second headline, a five-minute executive walkthrough, a 15-minute model walkthrough, the few figures worth memorizing, likely definition challenges, the main assumption, what the analysis does and does not prove, the next data request, and the role AI played.

Prioritize adversarial questions about causality, sample size, timing, accounting treatment, sensitivities, and actions. The owner should be able to explain the business logic and trace the evidence, not memorize every formula.
